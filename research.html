Research: videos I studied

Links to videos other creators made that I used to decide what to try. Numbers are as of Nov 20-21, 2026. Income and results quoted from other creators are their own claims, not verified by me. Listing a video is not an endorsement.

  • How To Start a GTA 6 YouTube Channel (Just Copy Me) (Romayroh)
    Published Nov 20, 2026, about 44K views when checked. Advice: long-form, faceless guides that answer very specific GTA 6 questions, on the theory that AI assistants cite YouTube videos; avoid compilations. His income figures are his own, the description promotes a paid community and affiliate links, his demo uses GTA gameplay footage, and one top comment says the commenter got two copyright strikes (unverified).
    My take: Good idea: answer very specific questions. Not copied: using game footage I do not own.
  • How To Play GTA 6 on PC RIGHT NOW! (Full Guide) (iNoToRiOuS)
    About 522K views on a channel with over a million subscribers. The description promises five steps and says the PC release is scheduled for 2027 (their claim). Rockstar's official page lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only (checked Nov 21, 2026).
    My take: I have not verified the method. Do not download any GTA 6 installer or crack: they are common scams.
  • The $30K/Month AI Faceless Shorts Method Nobody's Talking About (Full Workflow) (Sanji Nai-Chien)
    About 1.76M views (Jul 25, 2026). Claims $30K a month from ranking Shorts. The description says to paste in viral TikTok clips and links a sponsor tool. The income figure is self-reported.
    My take: Not copied: reusing other people's clips risks copyright claims and YouTube's reused-content rules. Original content only.
  • The Faceless Niches I'd Start Today (Before Everyone Else Does) (vidIQ)
    About 92K views (Nov 9, 2026). Six niches: horror survival, economy documentaries, personal-finance POV stories, historical autopsies, Living in [Country], and sci-fi/fantasy lore. It is also a product pitch for vidIQ.
    My take: Useful idea: check whether a format is still early before investing time in it.
  • I Built a Tiny PC From a Broken Phone! (GameRig)
    About 2.1M views, on a channel with about 278K subscribers.
    My take: Maker stories travel well. I have not summarized the build itself.
  • I Built a Home Server That Saves Thousands in Subscriptions (Switch and Click)
    About 1.26M views. Same territory as my own home server.
    My take: Shows there is demand for home-server videos.
  • 클로드로 만든 게임, 두 달 반 수익 현실 (광고비·AI 비용까지 전부 공개) (1인 개발 | 출시까지 갑니다)
    About 650K views. The description says 13.28 million won came in, and 2.06 million won was left after ad spend, AI costs, fees and VAT (the creator's own figures).
    My take: A realistic reminder: gross revenue is not income.
  • How to Invest As a Teen With $0 (Mark Tilbury)
    About 10M views. The description contains broker referral links.
    My take: General education from someone else. This site gives no investment advice.
  • 앤트로픽 개발자 리디아가 공개한 클로드 코드 최신 사용법 (민둥이 | 쉽게 배우는 AI)
    A Korean walkthrough of new Claude Code usage tips. I could not read its transcript.
    My take: About the tools behind these videos, not about making videos.
