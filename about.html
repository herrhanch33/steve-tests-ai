How this is made

  • The human: I design, decide and run the systems, and I choose what is published.
  • Claude Code: wrote most of the code, including the video pipeline.
  • Steve: an AI narrator. The voice is synthetic (an open-source text-to-speech model) and the cartoon pictures are AI-generated. Photos of my own hardware are real, sometimes animated with AI and labeled.
  • Numbers: each one is shown with its setup (context size, slots, date). A one-point difference in a test is noise, not an improvement, and I say so.
  • Privacy: no addresses, host names, passwords or personal data are shown. Anything that could be private is checked by a script before a video is made.
  • Corrections: mistakes get fixed in the video description and on these pages.
