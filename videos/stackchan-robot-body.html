I gave my local AI a tiny robot body (no cloud, 5 days)

A desk robot that talks to my own AI, no cloud.

Watch on YouTube

About this video

A small desk robot (M5Stack StackChan, my real one in the photos) now talks to the AI model running on my own home server. It hears a wake word, turns speech into text with Whisper, asks my local model, and answers with a Piper voice. Nothing goes through the cloud: I flashed my own firmware. Built in about five days, in English and Korean. The narrator is an AI voice; the mascot pictures are AI-generated.

The numbers, with their setups

  • No cloud: a robot that talks to my own AI (Everything runs on my own home server.)
  • How It Talks: 1. Hears you: wake word; 2. Speech to text: Whisper; 3. Thinks: my local model; 4. Speaks: Piper voice (Four steps, all on the home server.)
  • 1 s vs 2.7 s: speech to text: English vs Korean (Whisper small (English) and medium (Korean), on the server CPU.)
  • Stock firmware: no: the robot only talks to my server (Firmware built from readable config; the robot has no internet access.)
  • 5 days: from nothing to a talking robot (Nov 4 to 9, 2026. English and Korean.)
  • One Steve: same memory in chat, robot and voice mode (Memory stays on my server.)
